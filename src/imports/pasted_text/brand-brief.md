## 1. Brand Brief

**Invio Social** is a website + automation agency. The logo locks the two words together with a shared "8"-shaped infinity glyph replacing the O/S junction — this loop is the brand's visual signature and should echo through the site (automation = continuous loops, connected systems, seamless flow).

Design direction: **minimal, sharp, bold.** No clutter, no soft gradients-for-the-sake-of-it, no rounded-everything softness. Confident negative space, hard edges or crisp small-radius corners, strong type contrast, and one standout 3D moment in the hero.

---

## 2. Color Palette (strict — do not introduce new hues)

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Honeydew | `#F1FAEE` | Primary background — the "paper" the whole site sits on |
| Deep Space Blue | `#1D3557` | Primary text, logo color, nav bar, footer background |
| Steel Blue | `#457B9D` | Secondary text, links, secondary buttons, section dividers |
| Frosted Blue | `#A8DADC` | Subtle fills, card backgrounds, hover states, 3D element accent/glow |
| Strawberry Red | `#E63946` | Single accent — primary CTA button, key highlights only (use sparingly, max 1–2 per screen) |

Rule of thumb: **90% Honeydew/Deep Space Blue, 8% Steel/Frosted Blue, 2% Strawberry Red.** Red is a spotlight color, never a background.

---

## 3. Typography

- Bold, geometric sans-serif for headings (e.g. Space Grotesk, General Sans, or Neue Montreal) — tight letter-spacing, large scale (64–96px hero headline desktop).
- A clean humanist sans for body copy (e.g. Inter, Satoshi) for readability against Honeydew.
- Strong size contrast between headline and body — no mid-size mush. Headlines should feel loud and confident; body copy quiet and legible.

---

## 4. Hero Section (the centerpiece)

- Full-bleed Honeydew background.
- Left or center-aligned bold headline (e.g. "Automation that runs itself." style — sharp, short, confident copy) in Deep Space Blue, with the Strawberry Red used on one key word only.
- **3D element:** an abstract, glassy/frosted 3D loop or infinity-knot object (echoing the logo's "8") rendered in Frosted Blue / Steel Blue tones with soft specular highlights — floating, slowly auto-rotating on load, subtly reactive to cursor movement (parallax tilt, not full drag-control). Should look like automated "flow" — a continuous ribbon, gear-like loop, or connected node ring. Keep the model low-poly-meets-glassmorphism, not photoreal — sharp facets, not blobby.
- **Welcome/load animation:** on first load, the logo mark (the "8" glyph) draws itself or the loop rotates in from 0 with a quick easing snap, headline text lines slide/fade up in a staggered sequence (100–150ms stagger per line), then the 3D object fades/scales in last as the "reward" beat. Total sequence under ~1.8s — sharp and quick, never lingering or precious.
- Primary CTA button: Strawberry Red fill, Honeydew text, sharp/minimal corner radius (2–4px, not pill-shaped), subtle scale + shadow-lift on hover.

---

## 5. Structure / Sections

1. **Nav bar** — Deep Space Blue background or transparent-over-Honeydew, logo left, 4–5 links right, one Red CTA button.
2. **Hero** — as above.
3. **Services** — 3–4 sharp-edged cards (Frosted Blue tint backgrounds on Honeydew) for core offerings (e.g. Web Design, Automation Workflows, Social Systems, Integrations). Icon + short headline + one-line description each.
4. **Process/How it works** — horizontal stepper or numbered loop layout (tie back to the "8" motif — steps arranged in a loop shape rather than a straight line, reinforcing "automation cycle").
5. **Work/Results** — case studies or metrics in bold Deep Space Blue numerals on Honeydew, minimal cards.
6. **Testimonials** — simple, quote-forward, no heavy card chrome — a thin Steel Blue rule and bold name/title.
7. **CTA band** — Deep Space Blue full-bleed section, Honeydew headline text, single Strawberry Red button.
8. **Footer** — Deep Space Blue background, Honeydew/Steel Blue text, logo mark small in corner.

---

## 6. Motion & Interaction Principles

- Micro-interactions should feel *snappy*, not bouncy — ease-out curves, 150–300ms for UI, slightly longer (400–600ms) only for the hero 3D reveal.
- Hover states: sharp color shifts or 2–4px lift + shadow, no soft blur/glow overuse.
- Scroll-triggered reveals: fade + 12–20px upward slide, staggered per element, no parallax overload outside the hero.
- The infinity-loop motif can recur as a subtle animated divider between sections (a thin looping line that draws itself as you scroll into a section).

---

## 7. Do / Don't

**Do:** sharp corners or very small radii, high contrast, generous whitespace, one 3D hero moment, confident bold type, the "8" loop as a recurring subtle motif.

**Don't:** heavy drop shadows, pastel gradients everywhere, rounded bubbly buttons, multiple accent colors competing with Strawberry Red, slow/overlong intro animations, stock-photo people shots (prefer abstract 3D/graphic elements to match the automation theme).
